**What holds up (tested):**
- **Isolation between accounts.** Another account and the platform admin get 404 on every route: GET detail, versiones, aprobar, rechazar, estado, nombre, papelera. I tried ID variants 02, 2.0, 2e0, +2, 0x2, %2B2, 2%00, 2abc, -1, 0, 2^53+1 and 2.0000000000000001. Not-yours and not-found look the same, so there is no existence oracle. The list and catalog show nothing from other accounts.
- **Roles.** A lector reads but gets 403 solo_lectura on every write; an editor can approve.
- **Plan limits.** Of 8 parallel creates at the limit, exactly 4 succeed (with 1 already active). Restoring from papelera at the limit gets 409. A parallel restore + create can't go over the limit, because the handlers are synchronous and use BEGIN IMMEDIATE.
- **Transactions and SQL.** Errors inside transactions roll back. tocar() only receives column names from the code, never from the request.
- **Prototype pollution.** __proto__ and constructor keys in campos or capacidades have no effect; accion '__proto__', 'constructor' or 'toString' gets 400.
- **Odd payloads.** Arrays of 12k items, 30k-deep nesting and numeric strings are all handled.
- **XSS.** Playwright on iPhone 13 with injection payloads in name, instructions, motivo and every campos field: nothing ran, everything rendered with textContent, and the strict CSP is in place.
- **Error responses.** Errors return only an error key.

**What doesn't:**
- **High: CPU denial of service from crafted instructions.** 'sin aa sin ab …' (or 'without q0 without q1 …') hits an O(n²) loop at interpretar.js:259 and costs about 5.5 s per request (7.4 s pinned to one core). It even runs when the account is already at its plan limit. With the allowed 120 writes per 10 minutes, one account can keep the 1-vCPU server unusable for everyone.
- **Medium: project detail sends every version in full.** At 500 versions, one GET takes about 2.3 s and returns 7.5 MB, and GETs have no rate limit. With 15 clients that don't read the response, server memory reached 566 MB, more than the droplet's 512 MB.
- **Medium: the admin can read any account's projects through password reset.** Reset the password, log in as the user, change it, read everything. Nothing about it is visible to the user. Junior needs to decide how to handle this, since resets are by design while there is no email.
- **Medium: the papelera has no cap.** The plan limit doesn't bound stored projects or database growth: I measured 25 stored projects on a limit of 5, and one account could add about 480 MB a day.
- **Low:**
  - Values like {"toString":0} in any text field cause a 500 instead of a 400.
  - The editor role can pause, complete and trash projects, which the plan reserves to the dueno. Not reachable yet, since no route creates editor memberships.
  - Project IDs are numbered across the whole platform, so a customer can count other accounts' projects.

===== motor
SUMMARY: I reviewed the own interpretation engine on fase-1b (77cdfde), from a git archive in a temp dir; the repository was not touched. I ran about 200 realistic instructions through interpretar(), faltantes() and configuracionDesdeCampos(): Dominican job searches and sales, slang, typos, ALL CAPS, emojis, English, and many salary formats. I also confirmed some results end to end on a local server (API plus Playwright on an iPhone 13 viewport). The engine never crashed. But it often makes the approval screen say something wrong rather than asking.

Main systematic problems:
1. Negations are reversed. 'pero no en Moca', 'ni en...', 'no pequeños', 'que no sea zona franca', 'que no son bancos', 'sin Santiago', 'excepto los grandes' and 'but not to small ones' all end up included.
2. Capabilities: 'sin contactarlos', 'no los contactes' and "don't contact them" switch ON 'Contactar'. 'Solo investiga' still prepares proposals and emails.
3. Salary: 'entre 20 y 30 mil' shows 'Más de RD$30,000', 'no menos de 25 mil' shows 'Hasta RD$25,000', and phone numbers or years become the salary.
4. 'Santiago, RD' / 'República Dominicana' switches on the whole country.
5. The seller's own business or product becomes a target sector (ferretería, gimnasio, transporte, café). 'inversores' (investors) becomes generators and inverters.
6. Avenida or calle 'Duarte' becomes Duarte province, and 'capital extranjero' becomes Gran Santo Domingo.
7. Frequency comes from salary periods or 'Diario Libre', and the frequency supuesto disappears.
8. Job vs sales type is decided by a word count, can be wrong, and is not flagged.
9. Job titles swallow text after commas ('Empleo: administrador, salario entre RD$40,000'). English gives 'Job: as'.
10. Priorities run on to the end of the sentence.
11. Unknown places ('Stgo', 'SD', 'Cibao') are silently dropped when another zone is found, so nothing is asked.
12. Speed: a crafted 4000-char 'sin z0 sin z1 ...' input takes about 3.6 s in interpretar and blocked /api/setup for 3.7 s over HTTP.

Edit-form round-trip: all sector and zone labels, including those with commas or ' y ', come back the same. Only the product labels 'cámaras y seguridad' and 'agua y filtros' lose their product key when saved unchanged.
